Made in my culture style, built to become a brand.

CulturePunks is a collection of 2,222 rare and original hand drawn punks, inspired by NFT history and pop culture.

Every punk stands on a neon lime background and carries its own mix of skin, hair, eyes and accessories, plus a coin that shows how rare it is.

What makes each punk different

Seven trait groups, all drawn by hand and mixed across the 2,222 supply. Bars show how common each trait is.

Coins work like a rarity badge: the higher the number, the fewer punks carry it.

Questions

What is CulturePunks?

A collection of 2,222 rare and original hand drawn punks, made in my culture style with inspirations from NFT and pop culture.

How many punks are there?

2,222 in total, numbered 1 to 2,222.

What do the coins mean?

Each punk holds one coin in the top right corner: 1, 5, 10, 25 or 50. The 50 coin is the rarest.

Where can I get one?

Use the OpenSea button at the top of the page. The button turns on as soon as the collection link is added to this site.

Are all backgrounds the same?

Yes. Every punk sits on the same neon lime background, so the traits are what set them apart.
